720x348, one bit per dot: the 6845 programmed by hand, the world dithered by luminance, the cockpit drawn in densities — seams dark, plates half-tone, lamps burning. Pure 8086 assembly in DOSBox (WASM) via js-dos · source · built by Owlos

There was no colour on a Hercules and there is none here: what the card gives you is RESOLUTION — 720x348, twice a CGA's dots — and this game spends all of it. The picture rides 640x300; brightness is dither density, and the cockpit ships pre-drawn in those densities, baked offline. N still switches day and night: the night sky thins to sparse grain. The keys are OWL FLY II's, key for key — the full manual lives on the OWL FLY II page. Alt-Q leaves, ESC pauses: the world freezes, the views, radar and map keep answering.

On a touch screen the stick and the buttons you HOLD are drawn over the picture; the switches you press once — GEAR, FLAP, IFF and the rest, spelled out rather than lettered — are behind the keyboard button in the side menu.

There was no colour on a Hercules and there is none here: what the card gives you is RESOLUTION — 720x348, twice a CGA's dots — and this game spends all of it. The picture rides 640x300; brightness is dither density, and the cockpit ships pre-drawn in those densities, baked offline. L still switches day and night: the night sky thins to sparse grain.

KIT CATALOG - one line a file: what it is for, then its blocks. Made by MKCAT.ps1; do not edit.
LIB: include it after your way out, call its blocks (read only the header of the block you use).
REC: copy the fragment into your program and adapt. ART: how to draw a thing + a ready picture.
T_*: whole small games - START FROM ONE (docs guide KIT). CATFULL.TXT: every block with its line.
